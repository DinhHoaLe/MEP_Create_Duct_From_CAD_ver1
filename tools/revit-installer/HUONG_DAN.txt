IFC INFO - REVIT 2024

1. May can Revit 2024 (Windows 64-bit). Dong Revit neu dang mo.
2. Giai nen TOAN BO file ZIP vao mot thu muc.
3. Nhan dup Install.cmd. Neu Windows hoi xac nhan, chon Run.
4. Mo Revit 2024 va mo project. Chon Add-Ins > External Tools > IFC Info.

Cong cu doc IFC link da load. Chuc nang tao/cap nhat Duct va Air Terminal
co the thay doi model; nen thu tren ban sao project truoc khi dung that.

Chi cai cho tai khoan Windows hien tai. Khong can quyen Administrator,
khong can Visual Studio hoac pyRevit. Neu cap nhat ban moi, dong Revit
va chay lai Install.cmd tu goi ZIP moi.
